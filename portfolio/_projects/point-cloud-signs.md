LiDAR and photogrammetry give you millions of points and no meaning. For a city-development project I picked out the roadside signs and turned them into GIS features.

- Worked with photogrammetric and LiDAR point clouds to support 3D modeling and city development.
- Identified and digitized roadside signage in Trimble Business Center and exported the results as shapefiles for GIS workflows.
