The Hayward Fault runs right under the Berkeley hills, and the slopes above it creep. I used years of SAR imagery to measure that motion, then built a viewer so the results can be explored on the terrain instead of in a table.

- Processed multi-temporal SAR imagery in Google Earth Engine and ArcGIS to compute fault displacement and surface velocity fields, identifying deformation zones and potential landslide risk.
- Built an interactive 3D terrain viewer with Alpha Earth and DEM data, bridging raw geodetic data and digital-twin style exploration.
